# Lagos Life Reconnaissance — Research Package

Investigated 14 November 2026. Target: https://lagoslife.app/ (operator: Vatar).

## Read this before relying on anything here

- The research sandbox **could not connect to the site directly** and had **no browser**. Pages were read as text through a page reader.
- **No screenshots** were captured. **No responsive testing** was done. **No headers, bundles or network traces** were inspected.
- **The game itself was not entered.** In-game mechanics come from unofficial fan guides and press and are marked **S** (secondary).
- The public website, legal pages, ad-booking page and developer platform were read first-hand and are marked **C** (confirmed).

Details and the page checklist: [`00-investigation-log.md`](00-investigation-log.md).

## Headline findings

1. Lagos Life already spans Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, owns `naijalife.app`, and its Terms anticipate all of Nigeria. A nationwide scope alone will not differentiate a new product.
2. It is turning into a platform: third-party games, in-world advertising, partner stores.
3. Its documented weak points are client weight on poor networks, shallow business mechanics, thin regional identity, and account recovery.
